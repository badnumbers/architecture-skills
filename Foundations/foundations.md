## What this gives you

A universal mental model that travels across industries.

## How to use this plan week by week

You’re building the mental model that all architects share:

- how to think in trade‑offs
- how to reason about qualities
- how to separate “design” from “architecture”
- how to justify decisions

This is the part most engineers skip — and it’s the part that makes you look senior.
